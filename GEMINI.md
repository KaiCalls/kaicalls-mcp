# KaiCalls

KaiCalls gives a business a phone line with a secretary. The owner's phones ring
first. When nobody picks up, or after hours, Kai answers as the business, takes
the details and texts the owner.

Use the `kaicalls` tools to:

- Set up a business line: get a number or keep the one customers already call.
- Read recent calls, transcripts, recordings and voicemail.
- Look up and update leads that callers left.
- Change what Kai says on calls (greeting, business details).

Sign-in uses OAuth the first time a tool runs. Pricing is at
https://www.kaicalls.com/pricing. Never quote prices or plan names from memory;
point people to that page.
